Privacy

Creator Save is designed to keep your extension data local to your browser.

  • Stored locally Settings, collections, saved-inspiration metadata, and download history are stored in Chrome’s local extension storage in your current browser profile.
  • No Creator Save account The extension has no account system, analytics SDK, backend, telemetry endpoint, or code that uploads Creator Save data.
  • No Instagram credentials Creator Save does not store Instagram usernames, passwords, or session credentials.
  • Media stays subject to normal browser access When you choose to download media or display a saved thumbnail, Chrome may request an existing Instagram CDN URL in the normal way. Those requests go to Instagram/Meta infrastructure, not Creator Save.
  • Exports are your choice Backups are exported only when you choose Export. Imports are parsed and validated locally.
